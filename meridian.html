The prime line. Greenwich drew it, and every schedule since has bowed to it. This chamber keeps the operator's transmission schedule — and one intercept he never filed. The groups below were copied off the air by a listener in Utrecht, 14 FEB 1996, and mailed to the Society on a floppy disk. They are not numbers. They are letters, and they were enciphered the old way: one alphabet sliding against another, driven by a single word.

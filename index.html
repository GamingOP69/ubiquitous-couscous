WELCOME, OPERATOR.

You have reached the recovered terminal of K-7 HALCYON, a numbers station that broadcast five-figure groups on 6.840 MHz from 1963 until the night of 3 November 1997, when the operator transmitted a final message and pulled the master crystal. The station has never spoken since.

The terminal boots, but its memory is wounded. Four boot sectors were lost. Their fragments were scattered to the places engineers hide things. Recover all four, decode them, order them, and speak the boot word into the dial. The archive will open.

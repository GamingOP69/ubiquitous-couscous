SHORTWAVE PRESERVATION SOCIETY / NODE 3 / TRANSCRIBED 1998
================================================================

28 OCT 1997  0300Z  6.840 MHz  regular schedule. 5-figure groups,
                    24 groups, female voice. carrier steady.
29 OCT 1997  0300Z  6.840 MHz  regular schedule. 24 groups.
30 OCT 1997  0300Z  6.840 MHz  schedule SHORT. 6 groups only.
                    listener in Utrecht notes hand-keyed ID at
                    end of transmission. unusual.
31 OCT 1997  0300Z  6.840 MHz  no voice. carrier only, 40 min.
01 NOV 1997  0300Z  6.840 MHz  carrier only. deck B hum audible
                    beneath carrier (pitch fault, see maint).
02 NOV 1997  2314Z  6.840 MHz  OFF-SCHEDULE. two messages, hand
                    keyed. Society intercepts A and B on file.
03 NOV 1997  0001Z  6.840 MHz  final transmission. one word
                    repeated, then carrier drop. crystal pulled.
                    station never returned to air.
